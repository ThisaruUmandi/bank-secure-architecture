## Compliance

The design follows Sri Lankan banking regulation and international security practice.

**Regulator:** Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) guidelines.

**Sri Lankan legislation:**
| Act | No. / Year |
|---|---|
| Computer Crimes Act | No. 24 of 2007 |
| Electronic Transactions Act | No. 19 of 2006 |
| Evidence (Special Provisions) Act | No. 14 of 1995 |
| Payment Devices Frauds Act | No. 30 of 2006 |
| Payment and Settlement Systems Act | No. 28 of 2005 |
| Information and Communication Technology Act | No. 27 of 2003 |

## Privacy
- **Data minimisation:** collect only what is needed and store nothing unnecessary.
- **Consent:** customer data is not shared without consent.
- **Restricted access:** only authorised officers can access personal data, and every access is **recorded**.

## Audit
Both **internal** and **external** audits are carried out.

| Activity | Frequency / rule |
|---|---|
| Vulnerability scanning | **Quarterly** |
| Penetration testing by ethical hackers | **Annually** |
| Audit log retention | **7 years** |
| Audit log integrity | **Tamper-proof**: logs cannot be edited or deleted |
